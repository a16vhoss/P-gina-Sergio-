Pottery Class and Tequila Tasting

Groups of 8 to 10, hosted by local artisans

1 h Spanish and English Culture On request

About this adventure

A cultural activity, hands in the clay, no experience required. A local artisan teaches you the basics of hand-building and, within the session, you shape your own ceramic piece to take home.

Then comes the relaxed part: a curated tasting of high-end tequilas, with a host who walks you through the history, the production process and the cultural weight tequila carries in Mexico.

Groups are capped at 8 to 10 people, so the attention is personal. It is the ideal plan for couples, groups of friends and solo travellers looking for something authentic that does not depend on the weather.

Tour highlights

Take home your own ceramic piece
Guided tasting of high-end tequilas
Small group: 8 to 10 people maximum
Indoor activity: weather-proof
