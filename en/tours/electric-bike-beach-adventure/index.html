Electric Bike Beach Adventure and EcoFarm

Five hours, Mexican buffet with beer and an ocean view

5 h Spanish and English Cycling 2 departures · 08:30–11:30

About this adventure

Cabo San Lucas has plenty of terrain for beach riding, and on an electric bike you enjoy it regardless of your fitness: you decide how much you pedal and the motor does the rest. It works just as well for beginners as for experienced riders.

The route includes an hour-long visit to EcoFarm to see the local farming project, and there are photo stops with wide-open ocean views along the way.

You finish at an exclusive spot overlooking the sea, with a Mexican buffet, beer and handmade quesadillas. It runs five hours: this is the plan for the day you want to give over to a single activity.

Tour highlights

Electric bike: pedal as much or as little as you like
One-hour visit to EcoFarm
Mexican buffet with beer and an ocean view
Handmade quesadillas
