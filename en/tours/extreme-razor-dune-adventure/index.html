Extreme Razor Dune Adventure with Giant Taco

Two white-sand beaches, huge dunes and tacos when you get back

3 h Spanish and English Off-road 4 departures · 09:00–15:00

About this adventure

Pure adrenaline. The route mixes desert trails with two white-sand beaches and huge dunes that open onto views only the Pacific can give you. It is one of the most fun circuits in the area, and your guide sets the pace to match the group.

First time? No problem: if you can drive an automatic car, you can do this. We run the safety briefing before departure and the first bends are there for you to build confidence.

Back at base, freshly made tacos are waiting: chicken, al pastor and rajas poblanas, with homemade pico de gallo and spicy salsa. There is a vegetarian option too. This is the only one of our adventures that closes with Mexico's most famous meal.

Tour highlights

Pacific dunes and two white-sand beaches
Chicken, al pastor and rajas tacos at the finish
Vegetarian option available
No previous experience required
