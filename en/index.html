Why us

We operate what we sell

Best Cabo Adventures started in Los Cabos with a simple idea: let people see Baja California Sur with the people who actually live here. We don't resell other operators' seats, we run our own departures.

That means we know how the sea is running today, which horse goes to which rider, and whether the 3 p.m. group will catch sunset on the beach. It also means that if something changes, you hear it from us beforehand, not once you're already in the van.

We work with controlled group sizes, respect the protected natural areas and take care of our animals. The desert and the Pacific are our office, and we'd like them to still be here next year.

  • Our own departures, no middlemen
  • Local, bilingual, certified guides
  • Small groups and inspected equipment
  • Respect for wildlife and protected areas

FAQ

What everyone asks before booking

What currency are the prices in?
All prices are in US dollars (USD) and are per person. We show the adult rate and the child rate separately, with no fine print.
Do I have to prepay to hold a spot?
We don't charge online. You message us on WhatsApp with the tour, the date and your party size, we confirm availability, and we agree on payment before departure.
Is hotel transport included?
On land tours (ATVs, side by side, horseback, camels, zip lines and bikes) round-trip transport is included and the pickup time is arranged when we confirm. Ocean tours depart directly from the Cabo San Lucas marina.
Are there fees paid separately?
Yes, and we say so up front. Land tours have a 25 USD per person park access fee paid on site. Ocean tours have a 5 USD per person marina access fee, except the clear boat tour, which already includes it.
Do the guides speak English?
Yes. Every departure runs with a bilingual Spanish – English guide.
What is the minimum age?
It depends on the tour. Horseback and camel rides require riders to be at least 6 years old and 1.20 m tall; on ATVs, children can ride as passengers from age 7 and drive alone from age 16. Every tour page lists its full restrictions.
When is whale season?
December through April. In those months it's common to see humpback whales from Migriño beach during the horseback rides, though as wildlife it's never something we can guarantee.
What should I bring?
Comfortable clothes you don't mind getting dirty, closed-toe shoes for land activities, swimwear and a towel for ocean tours, sunscreen, sunglasses and cash for access fees and tips.
